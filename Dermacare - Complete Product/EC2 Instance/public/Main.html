How to Use DermaCare

🚀 Getting Started

1. Build Your Routine (Sidebar →)
Upload photos of up to 5 products from your daily skincare routine. Click "Add to Routine" and snap/upload pictures of your product ingredient labels. The AI will extract and remember all ingredients permanently.
2. Add Context (Optional)
Click "Context" on any product to add notes like "Use 2x/week" or "Morning only". This helps the AI give more personalized advice.
3. Chat with Your AI Expert
Ask anything! "Is my routine good?", "Any ingredient conflicts?", "What should I add for acne?". The AI analyzes your entire routine using a database of 6,222+ ingredients.

✨ Key Features

🔬 Ingredient Analysis
Every ingredient is analyzed for potency, timing (AM/PM), skin barrier impact, and sun sensitivity. Get detailed breakdowns of actives vs supporting ingredients.
⚠️ Conflict Detection
The AI checks 113+ documented ingredient interactions. It warns you if retinol conflicts with AHAs, or if you're over-exfoliating your skin barrier.
💡 Smart Recommendations
Get personalized product suggestions from 2,269 real products. Tell the AI your budget, skin concerns, and climate—it filters results just for you.
📸 One-Off Product Checks
Thinking of buying something new? Click the camera icon in the chat, attach up to 3 images, and ask "Will this work with my routine?" Get instant compatibility analysis.

💡 Pro Tips

📷 Take Clear Photos
Make sure ingredient labels are readable and well-lit. The AI uses advanced vision tech, but clearer photos = better accuracy.
🎯 Be Specific with Questions
Instead of "Is this good?", try "I have oily skin in humid weather—will this clog my pores?" More context = better answers.
🔄 Update Your Profile
Click "Retake Profile" in the sidebar if your skin type or concerns change. The AI adapts all future recommendations to your current needs.
📋 Important: DermaCare is for cosmetic skincare education only. We don't diagnose medical conditions or prescribe treatments. For persistent skin issues, always consult a dermatologist.
⏱️ Rate Limits: To ensure quality for everyone, you can send 10 messages per minute and 400 per day. Upload up to 20 images per minute. These limits are very generous for thorough testing!
