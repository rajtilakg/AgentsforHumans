Determining Your Skin Type

  • Normal: Balanced, neither too oily nor too dry.
  • Dry: Often feels tight, flaky, or rough.
  • Oily: Shiny appearance, prone to breakouts and enlarged pores.
  • Combination: Oily in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin) but normal or dry on the cheeks.
  • Sensitive: Prone to redness, itching, burning, or easily irritated.
